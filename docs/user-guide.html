User Guide

Old Man's Book Club — Last updated May 2026

Old Man's Book Club is a private app for reading groups. Members discuss books in real-time through text, photos, and voice messages.

Getting Started Library Chat Profile Admin Notifications

Getting Started

Creating an account

  1. Open the app and tap Sign in with Apple
  2. Authenticate with your Apple ID
  3. You will be asked to set up your club:
    • Create a Club — start a new club and become its admin
    • Browse Clubs — find an existing club and request to join

Creating a club

Enter a club name and tap Create. You are immediately signed in as the club admin with full access.

Joining an existing club

  1. Tap Browse Clubs and select a club
  2. Tap Request to Join
  3. You will see a Pending Approval screen while the club admin reviews your request
  4. Once approved, open the app and sign in with Apple — you will land in the app

If your request is declined, you can request to join a different club or create your own.

Library

The Library tab shows the books in your current club, organised by status.

Book statuses

StatusMeaning
CurrentThe book your club is actively reading
FutureUpcoming books on the reading list
PastBooks your club has finished

Switching clubs

If you are a member of more than one club, tap the ⇄ icon in the top-left of the Library tab to switch between clubs.

Adding a book (admins)

Tap + in the Library tab. Search by title to auto-fill details from Google Books, or enter them manually.

Chat

Tap any book to open its discussion thread. All members of the club can read and send messages.

Sending messages

  • Text — type and tap Send
  • Photo — tap the camera icon to take a photo or choose from your library
  • Voice — hold the microphone button to record, release to send

Message actions

Long-press any message to:

ActionDescription
SaveAdd to your Saved Messages
ForwardShare the message to another book's chat
CopyCopy text to clipboard
ReportFlag the message for admin review
DeleteRemove your own message

Saved messages are accessible from Profile → Saved Messages.

Voice messages

  • Play / pause — tap a voice message to play, tap again to pause
  • Progress bar — shows playback position and remaining duration
  • Speaker toggle — tap the speaker icon to switch between earpiece and speaker
  • AirPlay / Bluetooth — tap the route picker to send audio to external devices
  • Transcription — tap the transcript button to see an on-device transcription of the message

Profile

Tap the Profile tab to manage your account.

Display name and nickname

  • Display name — shown in member lists and message headers
  • Nickname — if set, shown in chat instead of your display name

Tap Save Profile after making changes.

Avatar

Tap your avatar image to change it. You can choose a photo from your library or take a new one with the camera.

Blocked users

Users you have blocked cannot see your messages and you cannot see theirs. Manage your block list under Profile → Blocked Users. Swipe a user to unblock them.

Joining additional clubs

Tap Join or Create Club in the Profile tab to join another club or create a new one while keeping your existing memberships.

Signing out

Tap Sign Out at the bottom of the Profile tab.

Deleting your account

Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the Profile tab and confirm. This permanently deletes your account, all your messages, and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

Admin Features

Club admins can manage their club's membership from the Admin tab.

Requests tab

Review pending join requests:

  • Swipe right to Approve — the user gains access to the club
  • Swipe left to Decline — the user is notified and can request again

Members tab

View current members:

  • Swipe a member to Kick them from the club
  • Swipe a member to Make Club Admin or Revoke Club Admin

Notifications

The app sends push notifications for:

  • New messages in a book you are a member of
  • Join request submitted (club admins)
  • Your join request was approved or declined

Notifications must be enabled in Settings → Notifications → Old Man's Book Club.
