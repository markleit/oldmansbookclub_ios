Privacy Policy

Old Man's Book Club — Last updated May 8, 2026

Old Man's Book Club ("the app") is a private book club app for a small, invite-only group. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and, if you choose, your email address. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one.
  • Profile information. Display name, nickname, and profile photo you choose to set.
  • User content. Text messages, voice messages, and photos you send in book discussions.
  • Device token. A push notification token so we can notify you of new messages when the app is in the background. We do not share this token with any third party.

How We Use Your Information

  • To identify you within the club and display your name and avatar to other members.
  • To deliver messages and notifications within the app.
  • To store your book club discussions so they are available across sessions.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on Microsoft Azure servers in the Norway East region. Voice messages and photos are stored in Azure Blob Storage and are only accessible to members of the club. Media files are accessed via time-limited signed URLs.

Data Retention

Your account and content remain in the app as long as the club is active or until you delete your account. To delete your account and all associated data, open the app, go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, messages, and all associated data from our servers. You may also request deletion by contacting us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
